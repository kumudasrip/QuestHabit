# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

QuestHabit welcomes contributors of all experience levels, backgrounds, identities, and abilities. Participation should be respectful, inclusive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Respect differing experience levels and viewpoints.
- Keep issues and reviews focused on the work.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help others learn.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Trolling, sustained disruption, or bad-faith review behavior may also result in enforcement action.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers should clarify expectations, model constructive communication, review reports privately where possible, and apply this policy consistently. They may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this code.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact a project maintainer privately through the reporting mechanism configured on the repository. No private email or reporting address has been configured in this repository yet; before publishing, maintainers should add a private contact route here (for example, a monitored project email or GitHub security contact).

Reports will be reviewed confidentially where possible. Depending on severity, consequences may include a warning, content removal, temporary participation limits, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces and public interactions where someone is representing QuestHabit or participating in its community.
